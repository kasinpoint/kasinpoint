About Me

I am pursuing a career in public interest law with a focus on housing equity and environmental justice. My work is driven by a belief that the law should serve communities — not just the powerful — and that access to safe housing and a clean environment are fundamental rights.

Outside of my legal studies, I tend to a small ranch in Hayward, California, where I raise animals and grow food. That connection to the land shapes how I think about environmental policy and keeps me grounded in the real-world stakes of the issues I care about.
